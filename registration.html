ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

RNSMUN 2026 OFFICIAL REGULATIONS

Academic & Student Eligibility

  • ✓
    Participation is open to students currently pursuing an undergraduate or postgraduate programme at a recognised college or university.
  • ✕
    School students are not eligible to participate.
  • ✓
    A valid college/university ID is required for verification.

Institutional Delegation Criteria

  • ★
    Registering as an Institutional Delegation (group registration) qualifies the group for the 'Best Delegation' award (₹10,000 & Trophy), subject to overall performance.
  • ✓
    An Institutional Delegation must consist of 9 to 33 delegates, with at least one representative in every committee.
