ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

RNSMUN 2026 OFFICIAL REGULATIONS

Academic & Student Eligibility

  • ✓
    Participation is open to students currently pursuing an undergraduate or postgraduate programme at a recognised college or university.
  • ✕
    School students are not eligible to participate.
  • ✓
    Participants must be actively enrolled in their programme at the time of registration.

Institutional Delegation Criteria

  • ★
    Institutional Delegation (Group Registration) enables the registered group to qualify for the "Best Delegation" award subject to performance.
  • ✓
    Institutional Delegation (Group Registration) should contain a minimum of 9 delegates, with at least 1 delegate in each committee; maximum strength being 18 delegates.
