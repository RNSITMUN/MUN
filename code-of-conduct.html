Code of Conduct

Diplomatic decorum, academic integrity, mutual respect, and inclusive diplomacy across all council chambers and conference grounds.

01

Core Principles & Equal Dignity

Foundational Standard

RNS MUN is committed to cultivating a safe, intellectually rigorous, and universally inclusive diplomatic forum. Every delegate, executive board member, secretariat officer, and staff volunteer is entitled to an environment free of harassment, intimidation, and hostility.

  • ✓ Universal Respect: Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, slurs, or bullying based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, caste, nationality, or physical disability.
  • ✓ Diplomatic Neutrality: Delegates represent the foreign policy of their allocated nation or portfolio, not personal ideologies. Contentious historical debates must be conducted with academic sensitivity.
  • ✓ Consensual Engagement: Socializing during committee breaks and unmoderated caucuses must remain respectful and consensual at all times.
02

Chamber Decorum & Parliamentary Procedure

Committee Etiquette

Chamber debate represents high-level multilateral diplomacy. Delegates must honor the formal rules of procedure governed by their respective council's executive board.

  • ✓ Third-Person Address: Delegates must address the committee in the third person (e.g., "The delegate of Japan believes..." rather than "I believe...").
  • ✓ Executive Board Authority: The Executive Board’s rulings on procedural motions, points of order, and speaking time limits are final. Constructive appeals may be submitted via Chits.
  • ✓ Chit & Note Passing: Formal note passing must be conducted through conference rapporteurs / logistics runners. Inappropriate, offensive, or frivolous personal chits will be confiscated and submitted directly to the Executive Board.
  • ✓ Electronic Devices: Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during all formal committee sessions. Laptops and tablets are permitted strictly for working paper drafting during designated unmoderated caucuses.
03

Dress Code & Identification

Attire Requirements

Professional attire is mandatory for all three days of the conference. Attire must reflect the prestige of international multilateral forums.

  • ✓ Days 1 & 2 (Western Business Attire): Formal suits, blazers, dress shirts, formal trousers, ties, formal dresses, or skirts of professional length. Casual shoes, t-shirts, distressed denim, and sneakers are strictly prohibited.
  • ✓ Day 3 & Indian Committees (Indian Formal): Formal kurtas with Nehru jackets, formal sarees, or traditional formal ethnic wear representing national cultural dignity.
  • ✓ Mandatory Delegate Badges: The official delegate credential badge issued at registration must be worn visibly around the neck at all times for campus security and chamber entry.
04

Academic Integrity, Plagiarism & AI Guidelines

Fair Debate Policy

RNS MUN takes a strict stance on academic honesty. Plagiarism diminishes genuine diplomatic debate and the collaborative efforts of fellow delegates.

  • ✓ No Pre-Written Resolutions: Working papers, draft resolutions, and press statements must be formulated during committee sessions. Introducing pre-drafted resolutions authored prior to conference start is grounds for immediate paper nullification.
  • ✓ Generative AI Policy: Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) may be referenced for preliminary background research prior to conference. However, verbatim AI generation of speeches, working papers, or clauses submitted to the Executive Board is strictly prohibited and easily detected.
  • ✓ Proper Attribution: Direct statistical data, treaty clauses, or verbatim quotes cited in committee must be factually verifiable.
Critical Reminder: Plagiarized documents or unverified resolutions will result in immediate disqualification of the signatory delegation from all Best Delegate and High Commendation awards.
05

Campus Safety, Substances & Facility Care

Campus Regulations

RNS Institute of Technology is an educational institution of excellence. All attendees must adhere to university safety policies and legal mandates.

  • ✓ Strict Substance Prohibition: Consumption, possession, or distribution of alcohol, cigarettes, electronic vaping devices, or contraband substances is strictly forbidden on the college campus. Any violation results in immediate police and institutional reporting.
  • ✓ Facility & Audio-Visual Equipment Care: Delegates must handle microphones, projectors, council furniture, and hall fixtures with care. Any deliberate vandalism will incur full financial damages billed to the delegate's college.
  • ✓ Campus Boundaries: External delegates must remain within designated conference zones, auditoriums, council chambers, and food courts. Entry into unauthorized administrative areas is prohibited.
06

Disciplinary Matrix & Incident Escalation

Sanction Framework

Violations of the Code of Conduct are reviewed collaboratively by the Council Executive Board, the Director-General, and the Faculty In-charge under the following transparent escalation framework:

Sanction Tier Violation Severity Consequence / Disciplinary Action
Tier 1 Minor Procedural Disruption Verbal warning by Executive Board; recorded on chamber minutes.
Tier 2 Repeated Unparliamentary Language / Chit Abuse Suspension of speaking rights for one committee session; forfeiture of award eligibility.
Tier 3 Plagiarism / Harassment / Unethical Conduct Immediate expulsion from conference chamber; delegation badge revoked without refund.
Tier 4 Substance Abuse / Violence / Severe Misconduct Immediate campus escort-out; official formal letter dispatched to the Dean / Vice-Chancellor of the offending delegate's institution.

Confidential Grievance Redressal

If you experience or witness any violation of this Code of Conduct, you have the right to confidential support. Our Secretariat Delegate Affairs committee and Faculty Advisory panel handle all inquiries with absolute discretion.
