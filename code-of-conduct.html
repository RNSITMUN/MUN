Code of Conduct

Official rules and regulations to ensure a professional, respectful, fair, and engaging Model United Nations experience for all participants.

Please follow these rules to ensure a professional, respectful, fair, and engaging Model United Nations experience for all participants.
1. Delegate Conduct 2. Committee Decorum 3. Diplomatic Conduct 4. Harassment & Discrimination 5. Dress Code 6. Social Media 7. Academic Integrity & AI 8. Restricted Substances 9. Venue & Property 10. Identification 11. Awards & Fair Play 12. Disciplinary Action 13. Final Authority
1

Delegate Conduct

  • All delegates are expected to maintain professionalism and decorum throughout the conference.
  • Delegates must treat fellow participants, Executive Board members, Organizing Committee members, volunteers, and staff with respect.
  • Any form of intimidation, bullying, harassment, or disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated.
2

Committee Decorum

  • Delegates must follow the instructions of the Executive Board and adhere to the Rules of Procedure of their respective committees.
  • Decisions of the Executive Board regarding committee procedure must be respected.
  • Delegates are expected to maintain proper decorum and follow the established rules throughout committee sessions.
3

Diplomatic Conduct

  • Offensive, derogatory, discriminatory, or inflammatory remarks directed at any participant or group are prohibited.
  • Delegates are expected to use diplomatic and respectful language during formal debate.
  • Personal attacks against other delegates are strictly prohibited.
4

Harassment & Discrimination

  • RNSMUN maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment and discrimination.
  • Discrimination or harassment based on gender, race, nationality, religion, disability, appearance, or any other personal characteristic will not be tolerated.
  • Sexual harassment, unwanted physical contact, threats, stalking, or inappropriate conduct of any kind are strictly prohibited.
  • Any concerns regarding harassment or inappropriate behavior should be reported to the Organizing Committee immediately.
  • Reports will be handled appropriately and, where possible, with confidentiality.
5

Dress Code

  • Delegates are expected to dress in formal Western or traditional formal attire appropriate for a diplomatic conference.
  • Excessively revealing or inappropriate clothing is not permitted during committee sessions.
  • The Organizing Committee reserves the right to address dress-code violations.
6

Communication & Social Media

  • Delegates are expected to maintain appropriate conduct on social media and in their respective WhatsApp group chats in relation to the conference.
  • Posting or sharing content that is abusive, threatening, discriminatory, sexually inappropriate, or intended to harass another participant is prohibited.
  • Delegates must not use conference-related communication platforms to target, intimidate, or publicly shame other participants.
7

Academic Integrity & Plagiarism

  • Delegates are encouraged to conduct independent research and prepare their speeches, position papers, and other submissions responsibly.
  • Plagiarism, fabrication of sources, falsification of information, or deliberate misrepresentation of another delegate's work is prohibited.
  • The use of artificial intelligence and internet resources must comply with the policy established by the respective Executive Board.
  • Delegates are responsible for ensuring that their submissions comply with the applicable committee's AI and internet-use guidelines.
8

Restricted Items & Substances

  • Weapons, explosives, illegal substances, alcohol, and other prohibited items are not permitted at the conference.
  • Smoking and vaping are prohibited within the conference premises.
  • Any item deemed dangerous, disruptive, or inappropriate by the Organizing Committee may be confiscated or prohibited.
9

Venue & Property

  • Delegates are responsible for maintaining cleanliness and respecting the conference venue.
  • Damage to venue property, conference equipment, or another participant's belongings may result in disciplinary action.
  • Delegates must follow all safety instructions and venue regulations.
10

Photography & Identification

  • Participants may be photographed or recorded by the official RNSMUN media team for legitimate conference-related purposes.
  • Delegates must carry their conference identification and display it when required.
  • Conference credentials must not be transferred or shared with another individual.
11

Awards & Fair Play

  • Awards will be determined by the Executive Board based on delegate performance, including diplomacy, substantive knowledge, participation, negotiation, and adherence to committee procedure.
  • Delegates must not attempt to influence the Executive Board regarding awards.
  • Any attempt to unfairly manipulate the award process may result in disqualification from consideration.
12

Disciplinary Action

  • Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in a warning, removal from a session, disqualification from awards, or removal from the conference, depending on the severity of the violation.
  • Serious violations may result in immediate removal from the premises and, where necessary, notification of the appropriate authorities.
  • The Organizing Committee reserves the right to take appropriate action in situations not explicitly covered by this Code of Conduct.
13

Final Authority

  • By registering for RNSMUN, all participants agree to abide by this Code of Conduct and the Rules of Procedure of their respective committees.
  • The Organizing Committee reserves the right to interpret and enforce these regulations in the best interests of safety, fairness, and the smooth functioning of the conference.
  • Participation in RNSMUN constitutes acceptance of these terms and regulations.

Organizing Committee Contact

Any concerns regarding harassment, safety, or inappropriate behaviour should be reported to the Organizing Committee immediately.
